# Reflection on my findings in *myDataJournal.js*

- Were your predictions right?
    - They were right to some extent.  I started flipping numbers around when I realized that I didn't know how to handle multiple values being the same.  I then forgot to adjust my predictions accordingly.  Things got out of whack.  I also predicted things I ended up deciding not to track.  I could have fudged these predictions after the fact, but it was a good reminder that I drifted from the original intention.

- What surprised you?
    - I was surprised that I was unprepared for this assignment.  I was also surprised at the level of detail I needed to convert a sample loop from a previous assignment to suite my purposes.  The amount of attention to detail requires is non-trivial.


- What was tricky or rewarding?
    - Attention to detail is paramount.  This alone was tricky.  But the hardest thing--something I was incapable of doing--was the Weresquirrel phi analysis.  This was completely beyond me.


- How did using your own data change things?
    - It made it more personal, certainly, but it also hemmed me by trying to honor reality.  Accordingly, I went way off track initially and put in moods that were too various to later correlate to caffeine intake.  This set off a wild goose chase where I stopped focusing on the code, and instead focused on how I could do a statistical analysis outside of JavaScript.  Bad idea.  I distracted myself to exhaustion, and blew past the deadline.
